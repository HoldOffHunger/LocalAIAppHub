Night shift

While the window is open, the Foreman works the active tasks top to bottom, each once per night. If Autonoma's model is on the GPU, it waits until the card is free.

Until you're back: it starts once nobody has touched the keyboard or mouse for 15 minutes, and the first touch after that ends the night.

Rest between jobs

After a task's turn, the night shift and free time wait this long before starting the next. A turn that came straight back (nothing it could do) doesn't rest, and giving the workers time by hand starts at once.

Stop time

In the rotation

    Saving moves the free time and the working task's stop time together. The worker restarts on the new stop time so it can plan its turn across the whole stretch, and it picks up where it left off.
